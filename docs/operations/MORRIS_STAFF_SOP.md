## 1. Access

1. Open the approved workspace link and choose **Sign in with Microsoft** with your approved Morris work account.
2. Look for **Morris workspace**. On a phone, open the menu. Use **Search workspace** to find a destination or project and the account icon to sign out.
3. Use the appearance icon to choose Light, Dark or System. System follows the device setting.

Project leads maintain projects and prepare drafts. The assigned Time operator confirms mappings. Staff with publication permission can approve mapped effort and publish content. The configured platform administrator handles deletion and protected settings. Ask the operations lead about missing permissions.

## 3. Set up and maintain projects

1. **Parent Project.** Choose **Create Project**, select **Parent Project** and enter its name. Continue through description, schedule and people. Choose agreed hour caps where applicable. Choose **Save Draft** while preparing the project. Find it in **Project Management**.
2. **Delivery Project.** Choose **Delivery Project** and select its Parent Project and Department. Add people and a client-facing description as appropriate. Select the exact discovered work order if available. The selection requests a separate mapping review. Save the project even if its work order has yet to be created.
3. **Departments.** Open **Departments** and use its settings action. Review names, order and visibility before saving. Confirm which delivery projects belong in the department.
4. **Hierarchy repair.** Open project setup and use **Update project hierarchy** when offered. Choose the verified parent and department. Check the resulting location in the wizard and save a draft. Review any existing mappings or reporting with the operations lead.
5. **Dates and venues.** In **Schedule and hours**, expand **Delivery activity dates and venue**. Pick one date, an inclusive first and last date, or each separate delivery date. Verify the day count. Keep one shared venue or enter a venue per date. Choose **Ongoing or unscheduled** when there are no delivery dates. The project start and deadline describe the overall lifecycle.
6. **Progress and status.** In **Project Management**, open the project. Record verified progress, dates, milestones, blockers and updates. Use a verified completion date when you complete a project. To reopen it, change its delivery status and review the completion fields. Check the visibility of notes before saving or publishing.

**Example.** Create Workshop Materials under the agreed parent and Training. Choose two separate delivery dates and a shared virtual venue. Save a private draft. Expect the review to show the agreed location, dates and people.

## 4. Review QuickBooks Time mappings

1. Open **QuickBooks Time Mapping**. Find the discovered work order by name and verify the jobcode. Open **Review mapping**. If work has yet to be discovered, keep the delivery record ready and check Integration Health after the normal sync.
2. Choose **Parent Project** for general engagement time or **Delivery Project** for work tied to a specific delivery project. Check the selected project and its parent and department.
3. Wait for the full impact. Review the recorded and eligible hours, entries with existing classifications or splits, conflicts, exclusions, and any hierarchy problems. If a refreshed review differs, reconcile it before confirming again.
4. Acknowledge the review and choose **Confirm mapping**. Watch the historical check. To recover an interrupted check, reopen the mapped work order and use **Check historical entries** or **Continue historical classification** when shown. Review conflicting entries in **Time Allocation**.

**Example.** If the Workshop Materials mapping review lists five eligible entries and one existing split, verify the split and confirm the link. Expect eligible entries to become staff classifications and the split to retain its assignments. Check the next normal synchronization for new classified time under the same project.

## 5. Classify, review and approve Time

1. Open **Time Allocation** and apply the reporting period. In **Advanced filters**, choose one or more consultants, work order, parent, department, delivery project, allocation status, mapping status or classification source. Apply the filters and check the scope and matches. Sort using a meaningful table column.
2. For a parent-only entry, open **Manage Time**. Verify the source parent. Choose a department first to narrow delivery projects, or choose the delivery project and confirm the derived department. Resolve uncertain mappings with the Time operator.
3. Keep the original QuickBooks Time description for reference. Use **Reporting description** for reporting context and **Internal note** for private staff information. Enter the full assignment or **Add split** for several projects. Check remaining time before choosing **Save allocation**. Review Activity History or Undo if a correction is needed.
4. To backfill several eligible entries, apply a precise scope. Choose **Select eligible entries on this page** or **Select all eligible matches**. Review the exact count, recorded and available hours, and the compatible destination. Choose **Assign selected time** and confirm once. Review completed, conflicting and retryable outcomes.
5. To approve mapped effort, use a single **Delivery Project** filter and apply it. With publication permission, use **Select this project’s automatic classifications**, review the selected effort and choose **Approve selected effort**. Expect the approval in allocation history. Clients see approved hours only on projects published and authorized for them.

**Example.** Filter parent-only time to the consultant, parent and period. Select the eligible matches and check their sum. Send them to the agreed delivery project. If a source revision changed, confirm the updated entry before making a fresh selection.

## 6. Prepare and publish reporting

1. Review **Overview**, the delivery project, its parent and department, and **Budgets & Delivery Health**. Recorded hours come from the source. Attributed hours follow the verified mapping. Staff automatic classifications await approval. Check these states separately from approved reporting effort.
2. Open **Draft Preview**. Review the wording, dates, figures and statuses. Check the visibility of updates and blockers.
3. Use the publication action after approval. Reopen **Published client report** and compare the correct period and project. Open **Presentation** for a briefing check.
4. Use the CSV action for the relevant report. Check the period, filters and ordering before exporting. Store any staff Time export in the approved Morris location.

**Example.** Review a month’s mapped training effort, approve the reviewed entries, publish the project update and verify the authorized report in preview. Check the explanatory paragraph separately from the figures.

## 7. Maintain the workspace

1. **Daily.** Check the last successful synchronization and exception queue. Review newly discovered work orders and correct straightforward assignment mistakes.
2. **Weekly.** Review pending mappings, project hierarchy, blockers, staff-only classifications and consultant identities. Use **Employee Mapping** to compare a verified consultant with the correct Morris profile and **Integration Health** to check sync status.
3. **Monthly.** Reconcile the period, source and approved totals. Review caps and drafts, publish after approval, check the client view and save the authorized export.
4. **Delete an unused draft.** A platform administrator opens **Delete project**, reviews dependencies, types the exact name and confirms. Expect the project to disappear from operational screens and an audit entry to remain.
5. **Handle protected history.** Follow the dependency guidance to review child projects, mappings, approved reporting or allocation history. Reassign children only after confirming their new location. Use a non-destructive status such as closed when the project must be retained.

## 8. Troubleshooting and escalation

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Work order missing | Confirm the correct customer scope and check the most recent sync. Keep the request pending until the operator verifies it. |
| Mapping conflict | Refresh the work order, inspect the revised impact and review established assignments before confirming. |
| No eligible source rows | Verify the period, parent, mapping and allocation status. Check existing splits or automatic classifications separately. |
| Bulk processing interrupted | Use **Check this bulk request again** or **Retry unsaved entries** when offered. Review changed records before making a new selection. |
| Published figures differ | Check the period, approval and project publication. Escalate unresolved discrepancies to the reporting lead. |
| Sync repeatedly fails or role missing | Send the route, time and non-sensitive error text to the operations lead or technical administrator. |
