## 1. Sign in

1. Open the secure dashboard link supplied by Morris Interactive.
2. Choose **Sign in with Google** and select the account you registered for access.
3. The sidebar identifies **Zagimē Anishinabēk First Nation**. On a phone or tablet, use **Workspace navigation** in the reporting toolbar.
4. Choose Light, Dark or System from the appearance icon. System follows your device setting. Sign out from the account icon when you finish using a shared device.

**Expected result.** You can open the approved reports and published projects assigned to your account.

## 2. Find your way around

| Tab | What you will find | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership summary | Status, department comparisons, deadlines and approved effort when available | Choose a count or chart item to explore related projects |
| Projects | Project Explorer with list or card views, filters and search | Filter results and choose **Open project** |
| Departments | Approved work organized by department | Open a department and browse the projects available to you |
| MI Directory | Morris team profiles and available approved contact links | Find a Morris contact and open the contact information |
| Presentation | A briefing based on the approved dashboard | Choose chapters and return to the dashboard when finished |

Use workspace search for a section or project. The breadcrumb near a project heading takes you back to a broader view.

## 3. Review progress and reporting periods

1. Check **Reporting period**. Use the month selector or previous and next controls. Open **Period options** where available to change the view.
2. Open a project to check status, assessed progress, approved milestones, dates and updates. Use its timeline where available.
3. In **Project Portfolio Distribution**, choose Departments, Parent Projects or Delivery Status. Each row gives the count and share of authorized delivery projects in the view. A count of zero means no approved matching projects. **Not assessed** means a completion measurement is unavailable.
4. Hours and completion are separate measures. A chart may show **Share of selected-period effort**. That is the selected month’s approved effort divided by the approved effort across the selected period. When filtered to a project or department, **Share of monthly portfolio effort** uses approved portfolio effort in that month. Choose **Hours** when available to see exact reported amounts.
5. A missing date, update, cap or figure may still be awaiting approval or unavailable for the selected period. Check the project and period before drawing a conclusion.

**Expected result.** The title, reporting period and filters explain which figures you are viewing.

## 4. Use charts, exports and presentation

1. Hover over available chart labels or focus controls with the keyboard to read their details. Tap or activate an available chart item to open filtered detail. Use a chart’s data table when offered.
2. In Project Explorer and department views, use **Parent Project** to narrow the results or choose **All parent projects** to clear that filter. Choose a sort order and check the result range. Use **Previous** and **Next** to browse more results. Check any filters set by a chart before exporting.
3. Use the CSV action offered by the current report or chart. The exported file reflects the authorized information in the selected scope.
4. Open **Presentation** or **Present this view**. Verify the reporting period before presenting. Navigate with the chapter controls and use the exit button or Escape to leave.

**Expected result.** The presentation, export and dashboard describe the same approved reporting scope.

## 5. Get help

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No projects appear | Check the period and clear filters. Contact Morris if you expected an approved project. |
| An update, date or figure is missing | Check the project and reporting period. Ask your Morris contact when the next approved update is expected. |
| You see an access message | Confirm you used your registered Google account. Contact Morris if it persists. |
| A chart changes after a click | Check the selected department, project or month. Use the breadcrumb to return to the broader view. |

The dashboard contains information that has been approved for you to view. Use **MI Directory** or your existing Morris contact for questions. Include the page, project and reporting period so the team can help.
